# Emission and the levy

Two features distinguish Epic's monetary schedule: emission steps down through defined eras and eventually
stops, and a declining **foundation levy** takes part of each block reward.

## Units

| Unit | Freemen |
| --- | --- |
| 1 EPIC | <Ver k="epicBase" /> (<Fn repo="node" path="core/src/consensus.rs" line={31} kind="const" name="EPIC_BASE" />) |
| 1 milli-EPIC | 100,000 (<Fn repo="node" path="core/src/consensus.rs" line={33} kind="const" name="MILLI_EPIC" />) |
| 1 micro-EPIC | 100 (<Fn repo="node" path="core/src/consensus.rs" line={35} kind="const" name="MICRO_EPIC" />) |
| freeman | 1 (<Fn repo="node" path="core/src/consensus.rs" line={37} kind="const" name="FREEMAN" />) |

Every API accepts and returns integer freemen. 0.1 EPIC is `10000000`.

## Timing

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Target block time | <Ver k="blockTimeSeconds" /> seconds (<Fn repo="node" path="core/src/consensus.rs" line={43} kind="const" name="BLOCK_TIME_SEC" />) |
| Blocks per hour | 60 |
| Blocks per day | 1,440 |
| Coinbase maturity | <Ver k="coinbaseMaturity" /> blocks (<Fn repo="node" path="core/src/consensus.rs" line={329} kind="const" name="COINBASE_MATURITY" />) |
| Cut-through horizon | 10,080 blocks (<Fn repo="node" path="core/src/consensus.rs" line={363} kind="const" name="CUT_THROUGH_HORIZON" />) |

## Reward eras

Emission steps down at era boundaries rather than following a smooth curve:

| Era ends | Height | Duration | Base reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Era 1 | 480,960 | 334 days | 16 EPIC |
| Era 2 | 1,157,760 | + 470 days | 8 EPIC |
| Era 3 | 2,023,200 | + 601 days | 4 EPIC |
| Era 4 | 3,175,200 | + 800 days | 2 EPIC |
| Era 5 | 4,642,560 | + 1,019 days | 1 EPIC |
| Era 6 onward | repeating steps | 1,460 days each | 0.15625 EPIC, halving each step |

The per-era values are in
<Fn repo="node" path="core/src/consensus.rs" line={104} name="fast_total_supply" />. Note that the base
reward is not what the miner receives: the foundation levy comes off it first. On a local chain at era 1
heights, a mined block credited the wallet 14.5792 EPIC, which is 16 EPIC less the 8.88% era 1 levy.

From era 6 the base reward is 0.15625 EPIC
(<Fn repo="node" path="core/src/consensus.rs" line={58} kind="const" name="BASE_REWARD_ERA_6_ONWARDS" />)
and steps continue on a 1,460-day period. The era constants are
<Fn repo="node" path="core/src/consensus.rs" line={46} kind="const" name="BLOCK_ERA_1" /> onwards, and the
reward is computed by
<Fn repo="node" path="core/src/consensus.rs" line={293} name="reward_at_height" />.

:::note[Reward steps are not halvings]

The era 5 to 6 transition takes the reward from 1 EPIC to 0.15625 EPIC, a reduction of 6.4 times rather
than 2. The node's `Status` response exposes a `blocks_to_next_halving` field, but the steps it counts
towards are not halvings.

If you are building a supply projection or a mining calculator, use the era schedule rather than assuming a
halving curve.

:::

Because rewards are computed with integer arithmetic, later steps eventually reach zero and emission stops.
The node reports a hardcoded `max_supply` of 21,000,000 EPIC in its `Status` response
(<Src repo="node" path="api/src/handlers/server_api.rs" line={86} />), and `supply` as
<Fn repo="node" path="core/src/consensus.rs" line={104} name="fast_total_supply" /> divided by
<Ver k="epicBase" />, so both are whole EPIC rather than freemen.

## The foundation levy

A share of each block reward goes to the Epic foundation rather than the miner, declining across nine
levy eras:

| Levy era | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 8.88% |
| 2 | 7.77% |
| 3 | 6.66% |
| 4 | 5.55% |
| 5 | 4.44% |
| 6 | 3.33% |
| 7 | 2.22% |
| 8 | 1.11% |
| 9 | 1.11% |

The rates are
<Fn repo="node" path="core/src/consensus.rs" line={257} kind="const" name="FOUNDATION_LEVY" /> over a
ratio of 10,000, applied by
<Fn repo="node" path="core/src/consensus.rs" line={260} name="reward_foundation_at_height" />.

The first levy era runs 120 days and each subsequent era runs 365 days, nine eras in total, so the levy
ends at height 4,377,600 and the full reward goes to the miner from there on.

Foundation payments use pre-generated coinbase data shipped with the node, one entry per day of the levy
schedule (<Fn repo="node" path="core/src/consensus.rs" line={199} name="foundation_height" />), so a
foundation coinbase is constructed from that data rather than by the miner.

## Fees

The fee depends on the shape of the transaction, not on network demand:

```
fee = max(4 × outputs + kernels − inputs, 1) × 0.001 EPIC
```

A two-input, two-output, one-kernel transfer costs 700,000 freemen, or 0.007 EPIC. The weight is
<Fn repo="node" path="core/src/core/transaction.rs" line={671} name="TransactionBody::weight" /> and the
base fee is `MILLI_EPIC`, 100,000 freemen
(<Src repo="node" path="core/src/libtx/mod.rs" line={37} />).

The wallet does not supply a base fee, so there is no fee market to bid into. Block weight is capped at
40,000, with inputs weighted 1, outputs 21 and kernels 3. A pool applies its own floor through
`accept_fee_base`, also 100,000 by default.
